## Way Through

In 2024 I got seriously concussed, and remained concussed for the next two years. Few people talk about long-lasting concussion symptoms (sometimes called post-concussion syndrome), but studies estimate that 10% to 30% of concussions result in persistent symptoms. I'm one of the lucky ones who fully recovered. After I finally found a treatment that worked for me, I built [Way Through](https://rcalcott9.github.io/pcs-website/), a website that summarizes the current scientific literature on concussion treatment. I'm always happy to chat with people trying to find their own way through (email: [rcalcott@g.harvard.edu](mailto:rcalcott@g.harvard.edu)).
